Support

Stallfeed copies your shop listings from the Etsy marketplace into a Framer CMS collection, so your own website always shows your current products, prices and stock.

Contact us

Email joesimo@mail.grokbot.com. We read this inbox every business day and aim to reply within 2 business days. Paid plans get priority.

To help us answer quickly, include your shop name, your Framer project name, your license key (the last 6 characters are enough) and what you saw, such as an error message or a screenshot. Never send us your Etsy password or your Framer Server API key by email.

Common questions

How do I connect my shop?

Open the Stallfeed plugin in Framer, paste your license key (or start on the free plan), select Connect shop and approve read-only access on the Etsy sign-in page. Then choose your shop, map the fields you want and select Import.

How often does it sync?

Paid plans sync automatically about every 4 hours and republish your site. The free plan syncs when you press Sync. Marketplace rules say listing details on your site must be no more than 6 hours old, so on the free plan please sync at least every 6 hours, or turn on auto-sync.

Can Stallfeed change my listings?

No. Stallfeed only asks for read access to your shop and listings. It never creates, edits or deletes anything in your shop, and it never reads orders or buyer information.

How do I disconnect?

Choose Disconnect in the plugin, or revoke Stallfeed from the apps section of your Etsy account settings. We then delete your access tokens and stop syncing. See the Privacy Policy for what we delete and when.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Plans are billed through Gumroad. You can cancel any time from your Gumroad library or the receipt email, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. For refund questions, email us.

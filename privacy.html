Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information Sim0 LLC ("we", "us") collects through the Stallfeed plugin, its backend service and this website, and how we use it. We collect the minimum needed to sync your listings to your site.

1. What we collect

  • Shop connection: your Etsy user ID and shop ID, and the OAuth access and refresh tokens Etsy issues when you approve Stallfeed. We request only read access to your shop and listings (scopes shops_r and listings_r).
  • Listing data: public details of your active listings, such as title, description, price, currency, quantity, images, tags, materials, section and listing link.
  • Framer connection: your Framer project ID and, if you turn on automatic sync, the Framer Server API key you provide.
  • License and billing: your license key and the purchase email and status that Gumroad returns when we check the key. We never see your card details; Gumroad handles payment.
  • Support: anything you send us by email.
  • Operational logs: sync times, counts and error messages, kept for troubleshooting.

We do not collect orders, receipts, buyer names, buyer addresses, buyer emails or messages. We do not use advertising trackers on this website or in the plugin.

2. How we use it

  • To fetch your listings and write them to your Framer project, on demand or on a schedule.
  • To check that your license is valid and tied to your shop and project.
  • To send service emails, such as sync failure alerts, billing notices and policy changes.
  • To answer support requests and keep the Service secure.

We do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising, and we do not use it to build profiles of you or your shop.

3. Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers needed to run Stallfeed, and only for that purpose:

  • Etsy, to read your listings through the Etsy API;
  • Framer, to write your listings into your project;
  • Cloudflare, which hosts our backend and database;
  • Gumroad, which sells and verifies licenses;
  • our email delivery provider, for service emails.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

4. Storage and security

Your Etsy access and refresh tokens are kept only on our servers. They are encrypted and stored in our Cloudflare backend, and they are never sent to your browser or saved in browser storage such as localStorage. The plugin keeps only an opaque session ID and your license key on your device. Framer Server API keys you give us for automatic sync are likewise encrypted on our servers and never sent back to your browser. Our API key for Etsy stays on our servers. Access is limited to what the Service needs, and all traffic uses HTTPS. Cached listing data is refreshed on every sync and kept for no more than the time needed to run the next sync.

5. Retention and deletion

  • When you disconnect in the plugin or revoke Stallfeed in your Etsy account, we delete your access and refresh tokens and stop syncing right away, and we delete your cached listing data and Framer key within 30 days.
  • Listings already written to your Framer project stay there under your control; you can delete them in Framer.
  • Billing and license records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require.
  • You can ask us to delete all data about you at any time by emailing joesimo@mail.grokbot.com.

6. Data breaches

If we learn of unauthorized access to your data, we will notify affected users by email without undue delay, report it to Etsy where Etsy data is involved, and take steps to contain it, as required by law and by the platforms we use.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or withdraw your consent by disconnecting. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or California law. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Children

Stallfeed is for business use and is not directed to anyone under 18.

9. International users

We are based in the United States, and our providers may process data in the US and other countries.

10. Changes

We will post updates here and email you about material changes before they take effect.
